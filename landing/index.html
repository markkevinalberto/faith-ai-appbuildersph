Why local beats cloud here

Health data is the last thing that should need a signal

Private by construction

Readings, voice and photos of prescriptions never leave the phone. The database is encrypted with SQLCipher and the key lives in the Android Keystore. No analytics, no crash reporting, no account.

Works where care happens

Clinics with no Wi-Fi, provincial roads, brownouts, a prepaid plan that ran out. Models download once; after that the internet is optional.

Free to run, forever

No per-question cost and no subscription. Dose reminders are local notifications that survive a reboot.

Grounded in your own records

Every number in an answer is computed by tested code from what you recorded. The language model only puts it into plain words, and an output guard rejects anything it invents.

Built to be safe

A companion, not a clinician

FAITH organises and explains. It does not diagnose, prescribe or change treatment, and the code enforces that, not just the wording.

A dose is never marked taken by itself

Only you can tap “Taken”. An overdue dose becomes “not confirmed”, never “missed, take it now”.

No dose advice, ever

Questions about doubling, skipping or stopping doses are refused before any model runs. Generated text with dose instructions is thrown away.

Numbers are computed, not generated

Averages, ranges, trends and conversions come from unit-tested code. The guard rejects any number that is not in the facts it was given.

Every target shows its source

A clinician’s target shows who set it and when. Otherwise you see a general reference scale with its citation, and nothing is ever called “safe”.

Escalation comes first

Emergency phrases and dangerous readings skip everything else and open a card with steps and a Call button. Thresholds follow ADA, AHA, ISH and WHO guidance, marked draft pending clinical review.

  • Glucose below 54 mg/dL (3.0 mmol/L), or 300 mg/dL (16.7 mmol/L) and above
  • Blood pressure 180 systolic or 120 diastolic and above
  • Oxygen saturation below 90%, temperature below 35 °C
How an answer is built

The model is the last step, not the first

  1. Safety routerEmergencies and dose-change requests are caught here, with no model involved.
  2. Your recordsReadings, medicines, labs and appointments for the active profile only.
  3. CalculationsAverages, ranges, trends and conversions from tested code.
  4. LibraryCurated articles with sources, found by keyword and by meaning.
  5. Language modelPuts the facts into plain words. It cannot add facts.
  6. Output guardChecks every number and refuses dose, diagnosis or “you’re fine” language.

Try it

Install, add the sample person, switch off the signal

The APK installs without a store. A fictional sample profile with 90 days of data lets you explore every screen before entering anything real.

  1. Download the APK on your Android phoneAndroid 8 or newer, 64-bit. Download it once, from Chrome, and allow installs from your browser if asked.
  2. Open FAITH and tap “Explore with sample data”Everything is labelled as sample data and no reminders are scheduled for it.
  3. Let FAITH set up her models on Wi-FiThe first-run set-up picks the right ones for the phone’s memory, about 1.2 GB for the full set.
  4. Turn on airplane mode and ask awayThe Ask FAITH status turns green while the phone is offline and the AI is still running.
